# Visual Validation Notes

The primary HappyMe routes were reviewed at a **390 × 844 portrait viewport** after the full native feature implementation. Today, Garden, Pods, Kindness, and You all rendered successfully with the shared warm-cream background, plum typography, marigold emphasis, coral actions, and persistent five-item tab bar.

| Screen | Result | Key observation |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Pass | The joy pulse, quick composer, audience controls, and recent moment feed form a clear one-handed hierarchy. |
| Joy Garden | Pass | The calendar grid fits without horizontal overflow and highlighted days remain legible at phone width. |
| Pods | Pass | Create and join actions are prominent, while pod membership and moment counts remain secondary. |
| Kindness | Pass | Received/sent segmentation is clear and the floating compose action does not obstruct the message card. |
| You | Pass | Statistics, favorite memory, appearance controls, reminder settings, and privacy actions use an iOS-style grouped layout. |

The generated launcher icon was also inspected at full resolution. Its marigold sun-smile, mint leaves, coral details, and deep-plum full-bleed background remain distinct without typography or an externally applied corner mask.

The secondary routes—full moment composer, moment detail, pod detail, and encouragement composer—were then reviewed at the same portrait size. Their controls remained inside safe bounds, long text wrapped correctly, modal actions stayed thumb-reachable, and the pod and kindness contexts were visually distinct. A recurring marigold “private sunlight” mark was added to major headers after review to strengthen recognition without adding visual noise.
